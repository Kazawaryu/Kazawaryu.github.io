Research Projects

  • 08/2025 - Present
    Traffic-State Reconstruction and Prediction under Partial Observability using Hybrid AI Learning and Traffic Flow Models
    MSC Lab, University of Georgia
    • Develop a hybrid traffic-state reconstruction and prediction framework integrating deep learning with traffic flow modeling
    • Design AI models to reconstruct short-term traffic occupancy and flow from partial observations in urban signalized environments
    • Incorporate traffic flow dynamics to extend prediction accuracy to corridor-level horizons and capture underlying traffic evolution beyond observed data
  • 08/2025 - Present
    A 3D Digital Twin Framework for Closed-Loop Simulation and Control of Autonomous Vehicles (Sponsored by DOE through ORNL)
    MSC Lab, University of Georgia
    • Develop a multi-resolution digital twin integrating SUMO and CARLA for closed-loop simulation of traffic, control, vehicle dynamics, and hardware-in-the-loop
    • Propose a five-layer (L0–L4) fidelity framework to quantify the sim-to-real gap across geometry, sensor signals, perception, and behavior, with predictive-validity analysis and gap-budget attribution to trace error sources
    • Build a ROS-free annotation toolkit for real-world multi-sensor logs (six cameras, 3D LiDAR) that aligns LiDAR-keyed frames and auto-seeds 3D bounding boxes, reducing manual labeling to box refinement
  • 09/2025 - 02/2026
    CDASim Upgrade to Support Cooperative Driving Automation Research (Sponsored by DOT through Leidos)
    MSC Lab, University of Georgia
    • Develop core components of a distributed co-simulation framework integrating CARLA–CARMA–SUMO–ROS2 for unified traffic, vehicle, sensing, and control stacks
    • Conduct component-level and system-level testing to enable scenario-based validation of cooperative driving automation (CDA) in simulation
  • 01/2025 - 08/2025
    LLM-Driven Traffic Trajectory Generation with Game-Theoretic Modeling
    Waseda University & SUSTech (undergraduate)
    • Game-theoretic trajectory generation for interactive multi-agent behaviors, validated on downstream perception and prediction tasks
  • 12/2023 - 08/2024
    Data-Efficient Perception under Sensing Uncertainty in Autonomous Driving
    ISUS Lab, SUSTech (undergraduate)
